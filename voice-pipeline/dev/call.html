1. Record your voice (optional)

The caller will speak in this voice. Record 20 to 30 seconds of normal speech, in a quiet room, talking the way you would on the phone. A short recording gives a weaker likeness. You can read this, which takes about 30 seconds:

"Hi, it's me. I'm just walking home from work and thought I'd give you a call. It's been such a long week. On Monday my phone stopped working, so I had to borrow my roommate's for a couple of days, and then on Wednesday I missed the bus and had to walk the whole way in the rain. Anyway, enough about me. How have you been? Are you still coming over on Saturday? Let me know, and say hi to everyone for me."

Without a recording the caller uses the pod's default voice. The recording is deleted from the server when the call ends.
